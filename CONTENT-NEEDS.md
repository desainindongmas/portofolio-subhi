# Remaining owner inputs for recruitment and project conversion

The portfolio and bilingual concise CV use supplied facts. No client outcomes,
team experience, production-readiness guarantees, or availability dates are inferred.

1. Capacity: hours/week for a new engagement; earliest start; remote/hybrid preferences.
2. Client feedback: approved quotation with name, role, engagement context and permission.
3. Outcome evidence: dated account insights, project milestones, or operational feedback;
   separate observation from causal claims.
4. Delivery evidence: actual brief, blueprint excerpt, acceptance checklist, testing record,
   and handover example suitable for sharing. Newly written frameworks are proposals,
   not retrospective evidence of completed practices.
5. KabarKelas: current release status and a dated description of demo versus production features.
6. Teamwork: a real example of coordination, approvals, feedback or handover.

Private client projects remain excluded. Do not add Enzy Snack, personal-finance
or DDM internal project records to a public collection.

The live Site's audience remains owner-private. This update does not authorize
public access or change sharing settings. Confirm the intended audience before
sending the Site as an external application link.
